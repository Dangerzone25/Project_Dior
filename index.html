06   /   THE ESSENTIALS

THE FINER
DETAILS.

Designed to be experienced. Chosen to express a feeling.

01

FOUR INTERPRETATIONS

Eau de Toilette, Eau de Parfum, Parfum, and Elixir each feature distinct scent signatures. Concentration alone does not describe how a fragrance smells.

02

CHOOSE FOR THE OCCASION

For an editorial starting point: EDT for a brighter daily profile, EDP for versatility, Parfum for intimate evenings, and Elixir for bolder moments. These are suggestions, not rules.

03

HOW TO WEAR

Apply lightly to clean skin, keeping spray away from eyes. Start with a modest amount, and avoid rubbing the fragrance immediately after application.

04

INDIVIDUAL EXPERIENCE

Longevity and projection vary with skin, environment, weather, and application. Verified regional bottle sizes appear in the product selector above.
