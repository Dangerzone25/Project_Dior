Details

Know before you wear.

Concentrations

Eau de Toilette, Eau de Parfum, Parfum and Elixir. Each is a distinct formula, not a stronger copy of the same one.

Character

Fresh and peppery through to warm, ambery and dense, depending on the concentration.

Occasions

Lighter versions suit daytime and warm weather. Richer ones suit evenings and cooler air.

Application

Apply to clean skin at the neck and wrists. Hold the bottle 15–20 cm away and avoid rubbing.

Sizes

Sizes vary by concentration. Check the official Dior page for current options.

Longevity

Longevity and projection vary by skin, environment and application.
